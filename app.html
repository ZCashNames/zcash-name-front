How it works

  • Get your personal name john.zec and use it to receive payments instead of sharing a long cryptographic address
  • Own and control your names. Hold, trade on marketplace, or transfer to others - only you can make updates

Benefits

  • No renewal fees. Your names are always yours!
  • Names are stored on-chain. Data cannot be altered or removed
  • Private by design. Your name reveals nothing about your finances.

Control

  • Update your destination address without notifying senders. They still be sending to john.zec
  • Change owner, but only with signing by your private key. Only you can do that!
  • List on Marketplace and earn for popular names reselling.
